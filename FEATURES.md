# MTC Compliance Checker — Client Feature Sheet & Outreach Pack

> **Automated, 100% deterministic quality verification for EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2 Material Test Certificates against Client Material Data Sheets (MDS) and ASTM / ASME / NACE standards.**

---

## ⚡ 1. Platform Feature Overview

### 🚀 Core Capabilities
* **Instant Multi-Heat & Batch Verification**  
  Automatically cross-checks chemical composition, tensile/yield strengths, elongation, Charpy impact, hardness, and heat treatments across all ladle heats simultaneously in seconds.

* **Automated Carbon Equivalent (CE) Validation**  
  Auto-calculates Carbon Equivalent according to international engineering standards and immediately flags discrepancies against mill-reported values.

* **Smart Unit Normalization & Standards Conversion**  
  Seamlessly harmonizes Imperial and Metric units for mechanical strength, test temperatures, impact energy, and hardness scales with zero manual recalculation.

* **Traceable Compliance Matrix with Clause Citations**  
  Instantly flags findings as **PASS**, **DEVIATION**, or **DOCUMENTATION GAP**, linking every result directly to the exact client specification clause and page reference.

* **Human-in-the-Loop Quality Review & Concession Workflow**  
  QC Engineers and Reviewers can inspect deviations, attach technical concession justification notes, override findings, and record formal digital approvals or rejections.

* **Centralized Material Data Sheet (MDS) Library**  
  Store, version-control, and reuse client specifications and international benchmark standards (ASTM A105N, ASME B16.34, NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 sour service, Shell, Aramco).

* **ISO 9001 & EN 10204 Audit-Ready Governance**  
  Complete, tamper-evident audit ledger capturing every document upload, rule execution, status override, and user sign-off with timestamps and user roles.

* **Role-Based Team Access Control (RBAC)**  
  Tailored permissions for Admins, Quality Engineers, QC Reviewers, Auditors, and Viewers.

---

### 📦 1-Click Deliverables & Communication Suite

1. **✉️ 1-Click Supplier Clarification Draft (Gmail & Outlook Ready)**  
   Instantly compiles an itemized, ready-to-send technical inquiry email listing all identified deviations and required supplier corrective actions for quick copy-paste or direct email sending.

2. **📄 Formal PDF Inspection Certificate**  
   Generates publication-grade compliance certificates complete with full heat breakdown tables, Carbon Equivalent results, reviewer notes, and digital sign-off blocks.

3. **📊 Formatted Multi-Tab Excel Matrix**  
   Exports an audit-ready, color-coded spreadsheet breakdown of all chemical, mechanical, and documentation checks.
